Privacy Policy

compter, for iOS, Android and the terminal · Effective 25 October 2026

compter is a bookkeeping app for freelancers: projects, milestones, GST invoices, payments, expenses and tax reports. It is built so that your records stay with you. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and what it never does.

Summary

  • Your records are stored only on your own devices, in a file called compter.redb.
  • If you turn on Google Drive sync, that file is copied to a folder in your own Google Drive. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Data travels only over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). On your device it sits in the app's private storage, encrypted by your device's own encryption.
  • compter has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • We, the developers, never receive or see your data.

What the app stores

Whatever you enter, on your device:

  • Your business profile: name, address, GSTIN, invoice terms and logo.
  • Client details: names, addresses, GSTINs and similar details you type in.
  • Projects, milestones, time entries, invoices, credit notes, payments and expenses.
  • App preferences, such as the theme and the sync method you chose.

You put this data in and you control it. Deleting the app (or the compter.redb file on a computer) deletes it from that device.

Google Drive sync (optional)

The mobile app can keep your phone and computer in step through a sync folder in your Google Drive. Sync is off until you choose it in Settings and sign in with Google.

  • What it accesses: compter asks for the Google Drive permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). It needs this broad permission only to read the compter.redb snapshot that the Google Drive app on your computer uploaded, which a narrower permission can't see.
  • What it does with it: to offer you a sync folder, it searches your Drive for compter's manifest.json files and reads the names of the folders that hold them, or creates a new compter-sync folder. After that it works only with compter's own files in the folder you picked: the database snapshot, the manifest.json describing it, and a lease.json that says which device has compter open. It does not open, change or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • Where the data goes: directly between your device and Google Drive, over Google's encrypted connection. It never passes through any server of ours.
  • Your Google account: the app uses your sign-in only to talk to Drive. It never sees or stores your password. It shows the email address you signed in with in Settings, so you know which account is syncing, but does not save it or send it anywhere, and it does not collect your name or contacts.

compter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to provide sync to you, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

You can turn sync off in Settings at any time, and remove compter's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

How your data is protected

Your records include sensitive business and financial details. compter protects them like this:

  • On your phone: compter.redb is kept in the app's private storage, which the operating system's sandbox closes to other apps. iOS (Data Protection) and Android (file-based encryption) encrypt that storage at rest while your device is locked with a passcode, PIN or biometrics. compter does not add a separate encryption layer of its own to the file, so please keep a screen lock on your phone.
  • On your computer: the terminal app keeps compter.redb in your user account's application-data folder, protected by your operating system's file permissions. Turn on disk encryption (FileVault, BitLocker or LUKS) to protect it at rest.
  • In transit: everything sent to and from Google goes over HTTPS (TLS), through Google's official libraries. compter makes no unencrypted connections and talks to no server other than Google's.
  • Your Google sign-in: handled by Google's own sign-in on your device; compter never sees your password. The access tokens it gets are short-lived (about an hour), are kept in memory only, and are sent only to Google. compter never writes them to disk. A token Google refuses is cleared at once, and signing out drops them.
  • Sync settings: the only sync details saved on your device are the sync method and the id and name of the Drive folder you picked, in the app's private preferences. No tokens and no email address.
  • Limited use of Drive: compter reads and writes only its own files in the folder you picked. Each snapshot is checked against the SHA-256 checksum in its manifest.json before it is used, so a damaged or half-uploaded copy is never loaded.
  • In Google Drive: your synced files are protected by Google's security, including encryption at rest and in transit, and by your Google account's own settings. We recommend turning on 2-Step Verification for your Google account.
  • No servers of ours: there is no copy of your data outside your devices and your own Google Drive, so there is no developer database to be breached, and no one at compter can access your records.
  • Keeping and deleting data: your records are kept until you delete them. Uninstalling the app (or deleting compter.redb on a computer) removes them from that device; deleting the sync folder in Drive removes the synced copy; and you can revoke compter's access to Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Reporting a security issue: please report it privately through GitHub security advisories.

Sharing and files

When you share or export an invoice PDF or a tax return, the app hands the file to the app you pick (mail, messages, files). What happens next is up to you and that app. When you import a file, compter reads only the file you chose.

What we never do

  • Collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • Show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • Sell, rent or share your data. We never have it to share.
  • Track you across apps or websites.

Children

compter is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and its effective date changed. The history of this page is public in the project repository.
