compter

Bookkeeping for freelancers in India who work for several clients, billed by the hour or per feature. Track projects and milestones, raise GST invoices, record payments, and get the numbers you need for income tax and GST returns.

What it does

  • Projects split into milestones, billed at a fixed amount or by the hour from the time you log.
  • GST tax invoices, export invoices under LUT and plain invoices, as PDFs with your logo and terms.
  • Payments in full or in part, with TDS. Credit notes to correct an invoice, refunds included.
  • Bill clients abroad in USD, GBP or EUR, with exchange gain or loss worked out for you.
  • Business expenses, with input tax credit when you have a GSTIN.
  • Each financial year's figures for income tax (44ADA and regular books) and GST.
  • The quarterly GSTR-1 and the monthly IFF, ready to file.
  • Everything posted to a double-entry ledger and saved as you go. Dark, light and monochrome themes.

Your data stays yours

Your records live in one file, compter.redb, on your own devices. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no tracking. To use your phone and your computer together, compter can sync through a folder in your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.

Get it

Terminal (macOS, Linux, Windows): download a prebuilt binary from the latest release. There are builds for macOS (Apple silicon, Intel and universal), Linux (static, x86_64 and arm64) and Windows (x86_64 and arm64), each with a SHA256SUMS file. Or build it from source with Rust 1.88 or newer:

git clone https://github.com/harisr92/accounting-tui
cd accounting-tui
cargo run -- --demo    # try it on sample projects; nothing is saved
cargo run              # start on your own data

iPhone and Android: the mobile app is in testing and will be in the App Store and Google Play soon.
