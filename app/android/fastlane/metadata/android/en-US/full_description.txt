compter is bookkeeping for freelancers in India: track projects and milestones, raise GST invoices, record payments with TDS, and get the figures you need for income tax and GST returns. Your books stay on your phone.

WHO IT IS FOR
• A developer billing an Indian client per milestone, with CGST and SGST or IGST worked out for you
• A designer working for a client in the US or UK, sending an export invoice under LUT in USD, GBP or EUR
• A consultant billed by the hour: log time against a milestone and invoice it when it's done
• Anyone filing quarterly GSTR-1 (QRMP) or the monthly IFF, or declaring income under section 44ADA

PROJECTS AND TIME TRACKING
• Projects for each client, split into milestones at a fixed price or an hourly rate
• Log hours on hourly milestones; the amount updates until you invoice it
• See what is pending, invoiced and paid for every project at a glance
• Archive finished projects without losing them from your reports

GST INVOICE MAKER
• Tax invoices with your GSTIN, the client's GSTIN, SAC code and the right GST split
• Export invoices for clients abroad, with your LUT ARN and the exchange rate
• Plain invoices when you are not registered for GST
• Bill several milestones on one invoice and give a discount by amount or percent
• Your logo, address and payment terms on a clean PDF invoice you can share on WhatsApp or email
• Invoice numbers follow your prefix and the financial year, with no gaps or repeats

PAYMENTS, TDS AND CREDIT NOTES
• Record full or part payments and the TDS your client deducted
• Foreign payments record the rupees that reached your bank, with the exchange gain or loss
• Correct an invoice with a GST credit note: refund the client or keep it as an advance
• Invoices are never edited after issue, so your records stay audit-ready

EXPENSES AND REPORTS
• Record business expenses, with input tax credit when you are GST registered
• Yearly reports by financial year: billed, received, TDS, expenses and GST
• Income on a cash or accrual basis for presumptive tax under 44ADA
• Export GSTR-1 and IFF files to upload on the GST portal

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-up: your data is stored on your phone and works offline
• Optional sync with your computer through your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder only compter can see
• No ads, no tracking

ON YOUR COMPUTER TOO
compter also runs in the terminal on macOS, Linux and Windows, and syncs with the phone app. It is open source (MIT): https://github.com/harisr92/accounting-tui

Light, dark and gruvbox themes included.
